## Evidence status

Before calling the paper ready, resolve unsupported claims, verify references,
inspect every result against its source and evaluation scope, compile with the
official template, inspect the rendered PDF, and conduct scientific review.
The audit script checks recorded metadata and local consistency; it does not
certify truth, coverage, venue compliance, or acceptance.
