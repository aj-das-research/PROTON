# Public paper-writing skills: comparative review

Inspection date: 2026-09-19. This is a bounded, task-focused search, not an exhaustive catalog of the web. Searches covered ML/AI writing skills, scientific writing, LaTeX polishing, research planning, and rebuttal agents. Read primary files from **16 repositories representing 13 implementation families**, after grouping related implementations. Retrieved 24 skill/prompt/reference files; some were read in full and longer entries in selected sections. No third-party code was executed, no output-quality benchmark of these packages was run, and no causal claim about acceptance follows. Commit-pinned URLs and license observations are in [skill-sources.json](skill-sources.json).

The target package already handles evidence provenance, venue variation, exact prompts, Overleaf safety, and honest result reporting better than many public alternatives. The highest-value next improvement is **better composition and explanation**, rather than another layer of mandatory audits.

## Seven concrete improvements for this package

1. **Choose a consequential scientific question before improving its sales pitch.** Target `argument-and-style.md` or a narrowly routed `story-development.md`. When two credible narratives compete, compare their reader need, precise contribution, strongest evidence, closest alternative, and material counterevidence. Select one with a reason; do not require alternatives for a settled local edit. Reframing after results must preserve confirmatory/exploratory status, primary outcomes, and analysis history. The current one-sentence thesis is useful but offers little instruction for choosing it.

2. **Turn technical exposition into a teachable sequence.** Target `paper-architecture.md` and an original `composition-examples.md`. For a difficult mechanism, move from a small labeled illustrative instance to the relevant definition/equation, explain its operation, then return to what changes in the instance. Check domain, shapes, conditioning, averaging, optimized versus fixed quantities, and limiting cases where meaningful. A reader should be able to execute the conceptual step. This strengthens the current brief instruction to explain equations in prose.

3. **Select the representation for the reader's job.** Target `figure-integration.md` or `paper-architecture.md`. Exact lookup favors a table; shape, heterogeneity, or tradeoffs favor a plot; an operation with meaningful state/order favors pseudocode; an invariant or objective favors a concise formal expression. One or two values may belong in prose. Use the existing external figure skill for rendering. The improvement is an editorial decision rule, not a second renderer or a figure quota.

4. **Demonstrate complete composition, not just correction.** Add one original worked example with an immutable synthetic fact packet, sentence/paragraph purpose, full introduction or method/results passage, and annotations explaining the choices. Include mixed outcomes and a nontrivial technical step. Verify that the prose makes no stronger claim than the facts. Existing examples improve three individual sentences; a longer example can teach how those moves combine without becoming a fill-in-the-blank formula.

5. **Calibrate author voice lightly.** Target `argument-and-style.md` and optional project-brief fields. If approved author-written passages are already available, infer preferred register, sentence variety, agency, terminology, and citation integration. Apply as soft guidance and do not imitate distinctive third-party phrasing. Do not demand samples, compute a style score, enforce quotas, or remove necessary uncertainty. Existing “preserve author voice” guidance lacks an operational method.

6. **Make proposed evidence change a decision.** Target `experiments-and-analysis.md`. For each important missing test, specify the competing explanation, minimal comparison, interpretable measurement, and how positive, null, adverse, or inconclusive outcomes would revise the claim. Separate the proposed plan from executed evidence. This expands the existing research-question/comparison/measurement checklist into actual research prioritization without authorizing new computation.

7. **Test comprehension at several reading depths.** Target `review-and-rebuttal.md` and `argument-and-style.md`. Check what a reader learns from title/abstract/displays; then the main narrative; then the technical appendix. A central result may need concise restatement at different resolutions. Preserve definitions and qualifiers needed at each entry point. Ask a fresh reader to restate the problem, idea, evidence, and boundary, then repair the specific failed explanation. This produces actionable revisions without arbitrary accept/reject scores or endless review loops.

## What to preserve or reject

Preserve the local Overleaf workflow, version-aware edits, official venue checks, citation entailment, exact prompt appendix, fair comparisons, meaningful failures, and conditional figure roles. Preserve useful drafting even when some evidence remains open. Do not replace these with mandatory human approvals, more scorecards, broad “AI-sounding” bans, fixed citation/figure counts, or confidence theater.

The proposed additions are independently written recommendations, not third-party manuals. Metadata, commit pins, and license observations support traceability, not permission to copy whole skill packages. Raw retrieved text remains temporary research material outside the target package.

## Research limits

The search may miss unpublished, private, non-English, unindexed, or recently renamed implementations. Search previews were used for discovery, then primary files were inspected. `shake/skills` was discovered but not inspected because its connector metadata lookup failed; it is excluded from inspected counts. Package code and bundled tests were not executed. Large libraries were sampled at the relevant writing entrypoint/reference, not read repository-wide. No assertion is made that all 16 repositories are independent, original, current-policy-compliant, effective, or licensed for unrestricted reuse.
