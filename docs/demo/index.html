2Meet the data

The model was prompted with two classes, normal and diabetic retinopathy. The stream also contains images it should flag. Click any image to send it into the stream.

PROTON+ never sees these labels; colours are only for you. Each stream opens with the clinic’s own images, then shifted ones arrive.

3Stream & detect

Images arrive one at a time. PROTON+ builds a prototype bank from the stream itself and flags images that sit far from it, even when the softmax is confident.

Settings & how it works

Domain-anchored prototypes: a mean-shift step over the whole bank finds the dominant imaging-domain mode; class prototypes weight their members by exp((e·μ_dom − 1)/h). Consistency admission: an image joins the bank only if max p ≥ γ and its distance to the class prototype is typical (d ≤ m + κ·s). Fusion: α·z(S_MCM) + (1−α)·z(S_proto) with online z-scores. “Flagged” (red ring in the plot) means the prototype distance is more than one standard deviation above its running mean, the online z-score PROTON+ fuses; the chip shows the full fused PROTON+ score and its rank in the stream. Streams open with in-distribution images because the bank only checks admissions once each class holds Kmin images.
