What you are seeing

Each image is embedded once by the frozen VLM. MCM scores only the softmax over the two class prompts, so a covariate-shifted image (e.g. ultra-widefield fundus) that the model classifies confidently is invisible to it. PROTON keeps a FIFO bank of confident embeddings per class, scores the cosine distance to the nearest class prototype and fuses both signals with a variance-driven weight. PROTON+ anchors the prototypes to the dominant imaging-domain mode of the bank (a mean-shift step), rejects atypical samples from the bank, and fuses online z-scores, which keeps the prototypes clean when confident OOD samples flood the stream.

Model: FLAIR ResNet-50 exported to ONNX and dynamically quantised to int8 (24.7 MB; embeddings agree with the fp32 model at cosine 0.99). Example thumbnails are from FIVES (CC BY 4.0), DeepDRiD and Imagenette; their embeddings were precomputed with the fp32 model.
