Watch PROTON+ catch the images the softmax misses.

A frozen retina model (FLAIR) runs entirely in your browser. Feed it a stream of fundus photographs: PROTON+ builds a bank of class prototypes from the stream itself and flags ultra-widefield images and non-retinal images that look perfectly confident to the softmax. No labels, no training, nothing leaves your device.

1

Images arrive one by one

Each image is embedded once by the frozen model and scored immediately. Click any thumbnail, or upload your own.

2

The bank learns the clinic

Confident, consistent images join a per-class prototype bank anchored to the dominant imaging domain. Shifted images are kept out.

3

Two signals, one verdict

Softmax confidence (what MCM sees) and distance to the prototypes (what PROTON+ adds) are fused as online z-scores.

Under the hood
Domain-anchored prototypes. A mean-shift step over the whole bank finds the dominant imaging-domain mode μ_dom; each class prototype is the mean of its queue weighted by exp((e·μ_dom − 1)/h), so a shifted cluster that sneaked in gets almost no weight.
Consistency admission. An image joins the bank only if the softmax is confident (max p ≥ γ) and its distance to the predicted-class prototype is typical of previously admitted images (d ≤ m + κ·s).
Scale-free fusion. The verdict is α·z(S_MCM) + (1−α)·z(S_proto) with online z-scores and the variance-driven weight of PROTON. “Flagged” means the fused score sits in the top 20% of the stream so far.

Model: FLAIR ResNet-50 vision tower (Apache-2.0) exported to ONNX and quantised to int8 (24.7 MB, cosine 0.99 to fp32). Examples: FIVES (CC BY 4.0), DeepDRiD (CC BY-SA 4.0), Imagenette. PROTON+ is a follow-up to PROTON; code and benchmarks at github.com/aj-das-research/PROTON.
